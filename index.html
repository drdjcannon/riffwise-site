Common questions

Why can't I add a song from Apple Music? Downloads from streaming services are copy-protected, so no app can read their audio. Use audio files you own, a video from your library, or a recording.

The chords look a little off. Chord detection is automatic and works best on recordings where guitar or piano is clear. Try Simple chords in Settings while you learn.

How long does separating the vocals take? About a minute for a three-minute song on recent iPhones. It runs once per song and is saved; you can delete the separated audio in Settings to free space.
